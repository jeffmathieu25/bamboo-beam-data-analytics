# Collaborative research and data availability

This project was developed through collaborative research. The portfolio presents the analytical workflow, methodology, and selected aggregate results. It does not claim sole authorship or ownership of the experimental dataset.

## Data availability statement

The underlying experimental data are not publicly available while the associated scientific work is being prepared. Access may be considered upon reasonable request, subject to prior agreement among the research collaborators and relevant data custodians, applicable institutional requirements, and publication arrangements. Data release is not automatic.

Requests should state the intended research use, the required variables and level of detail, the requesting institution where relevant, and the proposed conditions of use. Any access, reuse, attribution, onward sharing, or publication conditions must be agreed before release.

## Public repository scope

The repository contains analytical code without saved outputs, methodology, metric definitions, schema-only SQL, analytical queries, DAX measure definitions, and selected aggregate report views. It contains no specimen-level CSV inputs, Excel workbooks, populated databases, SQL observation inserts, or PBIX files. No general data-reuse license is granted by the availability of this portfolio.

Aggregate report views disclose selected research results. Their inclusion is distinct from sharing the underlying observations and remains subject to the research team's publication and disclosure arrangements.
